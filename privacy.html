Privacy Policy

zakomi assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

Read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). The app never sends, modifies, or deletes any email or calendar data.

How data is used

Data is used only to generate a private daily briefing for the owner. It is processed on the owner's personal server, may be summarized by an AI model under the owner's own account, and the result is delivered only to the owner via a private Telegram bot.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
